Why clean solar panels?

Help Your System Capture More Sunlight

Cleaning removes surface buildup that can shade the panel and interfere with solar production. Research from the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) has found that soiling can cause annual energy losses of up to about 7% in parts of the United States, and one NREL-supported study measured performance recovery of roughly 5%–11% after cleaning heavily soiled panels. Recovering lost production can reduce the amount of electricity you need to purchase from your utility.

A visible difference

Dust and grime do not belong between your panels and the sun.

Southern California panels are exposed to airborne dust, pollen, coastal residue, bird droppings, and dry-weather buildup. Our service is focused on safely removing that surface contamination.

Performance note: NREL research shows that soiling losses vary widely by location and conditions. In the U.S., annual soiling losses can reach about 7% in some areas, while an NREL-supported pollen study observed 5%–11% performance recovery after mechanical wet-brush cleaning. A cleaner system may therefore produce more of its available solar energy and reduce utility purchases, but actual energy gains and dollar savings vary by system, electricity rate, weather, shading, equipment, and the amount of buildup. We do not guarantee a specific increase in production or savings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Labor Day special include?

Regular pricing starts at $149 for up to 15 residential solar panels. During our Labor Day Special, receive $25 off your solar panel cleaning. Additional panels above 15 are $10 each, subject to ordinary access and site conditions.

Do you use RO/DI purified water?

Yes. We use RO/DI (Reverse Osmosis/Deionized) water for cleaning solar panels. This helps minimize mineral spotting and residue while providing a spot-free rinse without harsh chemicals.

Can you guarantee lower electric bills?

No. Cleaning removes surface buildup, but production and bills depend on many factors, including weather, system design, shading, equipment condition, utility rates, and household energy use.

Do you service both Orange and Los Angeles Counties?

Yes. We serve Orange County, Los Angeles County, and selected surrounding communities. Travel or access charges may apply outside our normal service area.
