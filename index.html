Frequently Asked Questions

What does the $249 special include?

The special covers professional exterior cleaning of up to 25 residential solar panels under ordinary access and site conditions. Additional panels are $10 each above 25.

Do you use RO/DI purified water?

Yes. We use RO/DI (Reverse Osmosis/Deionized) water for cleaning solar panels. This helps minimize mineral spotting and residue while providing a spot-free rinse without harsh chemicals.

Can you guarantee lower electric bills?

No. Cleaning removes surface buildup, but production and bills depend on many factors, including weather, system design, shading, equipment condition, utility rates, and household energy use.

Do you service both Orange and Los Angeles Counties?

Yes. We serve Orange County, Los Angeles County, and selected surrounding communities. Travel or access charges may apply outside our normal service area.
